Rare Opportunity to Fish in a Private Location

June 14, 2026

I was recently granted permission for one day to fish a body of water on a worksite that had mysteriously gotten fish introduced to it, apparent because they were frequently visible from the surface of the clear water. Very few have been allowed to fish here because of the legal liability of the worksite and the fact that fishermen could easily get in the way of the ongoing work. Lucky for us there was going to be a day where work was shut down in order for a survey crew to do some measuring and that gave us the opportunity to fish for a few hours.

The water was a very pretty blue and clear enough that you could easily see groups of fish just below the surface a foot or so deep. I started out jigging with a plastic bait but after seeing the fish just watch my bait pass I switched to a topwater chatter and immediately started getting strikes.

I caught quite a few smallmouth but nothing very big, mostly 1 pounders but it was a whole lot of fun. Glad I had the chance to do it.
