Welcome to My Fishing Blog

Hi, I'm Marko. As much as I love to fish, I have not been doing it nearly enough recently. I created this blog to share my fishing experiences during the summer of 2026 and beyond, while also giving myself another reason to get outside and fish. I live in Northwest Indiana, but I will sometimes travel quite a distance for a good day of fishing. You can expect to see some of my best catches, favorite spots, equipment reviews, and more.
