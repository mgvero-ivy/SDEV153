About Me

A Little Background

Hi, my name is Marko and I've been fishing for longer than I can remember. I do most of my fishing in Northwest Indiana and the surrounding area but I also like to trek out for a fishing trip whenever I get the chance.

What's This Blog's Purpose?

This blog serves a few purposes for me. First I just thought it would be fun to share some of my experiences fishing over the summer. More importantly it gives me an excuse to go out and fish more often because I need to have something to post about!

What Am I Going to Post Here?

  • Brief reports of my fishing outings
  • Photos of locations and catches
  • Information about bait and equipment and the occasional review
  • And any other fishing related tidbit that I stumble across

Responsible Fishing

Fishing is a lifelong passion of mine and as such preserving our waters and healthy fish populations is something I hope to encourage. Please remember to always leave any fishing spot just as you found it and pick up any trash others may have left behind. Always be aware of your local and state regulations, obey bag limits, never keep a fish you won't eat, and avoid catch and release of invasive species.
It is up to us to preserve our waters for the future.
