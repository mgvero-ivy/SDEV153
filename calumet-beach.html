Calumet Beach Lake Michigan

Memorial Day Weekend 2026

For the last few years I've gone with some friends to fish the pier at Calumet Park Beach in Chicago, not because the fishing is particularly good but it became a tradition and so we do it. The pier is nice and wide, more than enough space to bring a grill out and cook while fishing and not block anyone else from walking the length which is a nice bonus. You will want to bring some food to cook with you because you're unlikely to catch anything worth turning into a meal.

We face a problem that gets worse year after year here. That is the growing population of invasive goby outcompeting the native fish. It is possible to get your hook into some bluegill and smallmouth but far more often than not your bait will be hit by the goby that have become ingrained in the area. By the end of the day every year we end up leaving a pile of goby for the gulls to eat because even if I was allowed to throw them back I wouldn't.

Still I enjoy our yearly outing, I like any chance to get a line in the water and grill with my friends. Sure the fishings tough but that adds to the excitement when you finally do get you hook in something good.
